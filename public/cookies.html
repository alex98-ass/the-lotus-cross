Cookies

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Short version: there is no cookie banner because there are no tracking cookies. We do not use analytics, advertising pixels, social trackers or third-party cookies. Nothing about you is shared with an advertiser, because there is no advertiser.

What is stored in your browser

NameTypePurposeLifetime
lotus_tokenlocal storageKeeps you signed in. Without it you would have to log in on every page.Until you sign out, or 7 days
lotus_userlocal storageRemembers your name and rank so the portal can greet you immediately.Until you sign out

Both are strictly necessary for the service you asked for. UK law (PECR) does not require consent for storage that is essential to provide a service you requested — which is why there is no banner.

Fonts and images

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. That request tells Google your IP address, as any request to any server does. No cookie is set by it. If you would rather avoid it, a browser extension that blocks third-party requests will stop it, and the site will still work.

If we ever add analytics

If we add measurement, it will be a privacy-friendly, cookie-free tool (something like Plausible or a self-hosted counter), and this page will be updated first. We will never add advertising trackers.

Turning storage off

You can clear this data at any time from your browser settings. If you delete lotus_token, you will simply be signed out.
