Our Vision

One Light, Many Lanterns

For too long, the great spiritual traditions of humanity have been treated as separate, competing paths — as if the same light could not shine through different lanterns. The Lotus Cross was born from a simple, radical conviction: that all of them are true, and that each one carries a piece of the eternal truth that no single voice could ever hold alone.


We do not ask you to abandon your faith. We ask you to expand it. To see that the grace of Christ, the compassion of the Buddha, the cosmic wisdom of the Vedas, and the moral law of karma are not contradictions — they are harmonics of the same sacred chord. Together, they form a path older than any of them alone: the path of love, awareness, and consequence.


This is not a religion of conversion. It is a religion of convergence. You are not asked to kneel — you are asked to awaken.

The Three Paths

The Foundations of Our Faith

✝

The Way of Love

Christianity

Christ taught that the greatest commandment is love — love of God, love of neighbour, love of enemy. We hold this teaching as the emotional heart of our practice: that compassion is not a feeling but a way of being, and that grace is real, freely given, and transformative. We study the Sermon on the Mount as a living manual for the soul.

☸

The Way of Awareness

Buddhism

The Buddha taught that suffering arises from attachment and ignorance, and that liberation comes through mindful awareness of the present moment. We hold this as the mental foundation of our path — meditation, contemplation, and the clear seeing of reality as it is, not as we wish it to be. Stillness is our medicine.

🕉

The Way of Order

Hinduism

The Vedas teach that the universe is governed by Dharma — a cosmic order, a sacred structure within which all beings have their place and duty. We honour this as the spiritual framework of our path: that there is a design to existence, that every soul is an expression of the same divine consciousness, and that our lives are sacred participation in that order.

The Unbreakable Law

Karma

"As you sow, so shall you reap."
"What you do to the least of these, you do to Me."
"Every action leaves a mark upon the fabric of reality."

— Christ · Buddha · The Vedas

Karma is the bridge that unites our three paths. It is the moral physics of the soul — the understanding that no action is ever lost, that kindness given returns, that harm done remains, and that the life you build tomorrow is being shaped by the choices you make today. We do not fear karma. We honour it — as proof that the universe is just, that our choices matter, and that we are the architects of our own spiritual destiny.

Ranks & Progression

Every member walks the same path — but not at the same pace. Your rank reflects your commitment, not your worth. All who walk are equal; some have simply walked longer.

🌱

Seeker

Rank 0 · Entry

Newly enrolled. Learning the Three Paths. Completing daily tasks. Building the habit of practice. 7 completed tasks to advance.

🌿

Initiate

Rank 1 · Committed

Has completed 7 daily tasks and taken the Vow of Harmony. Studying scripture, mentoring newer Seekers, contributing to community life.

🔥

Disciple

Rank 2 · Dedicated

30+ tasks completed. Leads study circles. Organises community service. Begins teaching the basics to new members.

⭐

Elder

Rank 3 · Leader

90+ tasks completed. Guides the community. Resolves conflicts. Represents The Lotus Cross in interfaith dialogue.

✦

Guide

Rank 4 · Teacher

The highest rank. Guides Elders. Shapes the direction of the community. A keeper of the teaching. Earned through years of devoted service.
