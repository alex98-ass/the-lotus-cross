Our Mission

Where the paths converge into one.

The Lotus Cross is a spiritual community that draws wisdom from the great traditions of the world — Christianity, Buddhism and Hinduism — and unites them into a single, living path of love, awareness and right action.

What We Believe

No single tradition holds the whole truth. Each holds a facet of it.

We are not here to replace your faith, but to deepen it. We believe the teachings of the Buddha, the wisdom of the Vedas, and the love of Christ are not in conflict — they are the same river, seen from different banks. The Lotus Cross is the meeting place.

The Way of the One Lotus

Our living synthesis — a path woven from the deepest threads of three ancient traditions.

☸️

The One Reality

Beneath every name. Brahman and God are the same ocean; the Buddha pointed to it by silence. We call it the One Source — not to limit it, but to honour it from every shore. The real is one; the wise speak of it in many ways.

🔥

Karma & Grace

We hold both as true at once. Every action plants a seed — this is karma, and we own it fully. And yet renewal can arrive unearned, as forgiveness and mercy — this is grace. The intoxicating paradox: you reap what you sow, and you are still loved beyond reaping.

🪷

The Threefold Way

One road, three tracks, walked together. Love (agape and karuna) — to give oneself, to serve the suffering. Awareness — to observe the mind, to untie the knot of the self. Right Action — to live one's dharma in the world. None is complete alone; all three together make a human being whole.

🌅

Suffering & Transfiguration

Suffering is real, universal, and rooted in attachment to an impermanent reality — here we trust the Buddha's diagnosis. Yet the suffering self can be transfigured, reborn into greater love — here we receive the Christian promise. Diagnosis and resurrection: we do not choose between them; we hold the cup and let it overflow.

🌍

All Is Connected

If all beings are one at the deepest level — the teaching of advaita — then loving your neighbour as yourself is not a rule but a description of reality. Compassion ceases to be duty and becomes the obvious truth of interdependence.

🧭

Practice, Daily

Philosophy that is not walked is noise. Our daily practice unites them: a period of stillness (meditation), a moment of gratitude and surrender (prayer), and one deliberate act of service (karma in motion). In this rhythm the synthesis becomes a life.

The Three Paths

One river, three currents. Every member walks all three.

💛

The Way of Love

Compassion in action. Kindness to every being. Service to those who suffer. Love is not a feeling but a discipline, practised daily.

🧘

The Way of Awareness

Meditation, mindfulness and self-knowledge. To see clearly — our own minds first — so that we may act without illusion.

⚖️

The Way of Right Action

Justice and integrity. To align our lives with what is good, to speak truth, and to build a world where all may flourish.

Our Values

The principles that guide our community.

  • ✦
    Respect all paths No tradition is superior. We honour every sincere search for truth.
  • ✦
    No proselytising We share experience, never impose belief. Faith is offered, never forced.
  • ✦
    Kindness first Disagreement is inevitable; disrespect is not. We disagree without wounding.
  • ✦
    Service over status Ranks exist for organisation, not hierarchy. The greatest among us is the one who serves.
  • ✦
    Voluntary always You may leave at any time. No obligation, no guilt, ever.
  • ✦
    Justice inseparable from spirit A truly awakened person cannot ignore the suffering of others.

Our Vision

What we are building toward.

A world where the mystic and the activist are the same person. Where meditation leads to action and action is rooted in stillness. Where people of every faith — and of no faith — walk together in mutual respect, deepening their own path while honouring the paths of others. We are building a community of practice: daily disciplines, genuine fellowship, and a shared commitment to love, awareness and justice.

"The lotus grows in mud, yet rises unstained. The cross bears weight, yet points to the sky. The path is not away from the world, but through it." — The Lotus Cross
