Policies

Everything that governs how this place is run, in plain English. No clauses designed to be skimmed past. If something here is unclear, write to us and we will rewrite it.

Money

We take money for exactly three things: written guides and printable files, objects made to order, and monthly membership. Nothing else. There is no “energy” product, no supplement, no object sold as lucky, and no payment that unlocks a rank.

Prices

All prices are in pounds sterling and include VAT where it applies. Postage is shown separately before you pay. We do not add fees at checkout, and we do not use countdown timers or invented scarcity — if something is limited, it is limited by how many we can physically make that month, and we say so.

Payments

Card payments are handled by our payment processor. We never see or store your card number. Subscriptions renew on the same day each month until you cancel.

Refunds

Digital files are the one thing we cannot take back. By downloading a guide, a journal or a printable sheet, you agree that the 14-day right to cancel under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 no longer applies to that file — this is the standard digital-content exception, and we ask you to confirm it at checkout.

If a file is wrong

If a file will not open, is missing pages, or is not what the page described, write to us within 30 days. We will fix it or refund it. We do not ask you to prove anything beyond telling us what happened.

Physical objects

You may return anything made to order within 14 days of delivery, unused, for a refund of the item price. Return postage is yours unless the item arrived damaged or was not what you ordered — in that case we pay, and we replace it without asking for photographs of packaging.

Membership

Cancel any time; access continues to the end of the period you have paid for. We do not pro-rate part-months. If you were charged in error, we refund it in full.

Membership

There are two paid plans and a free one. What each includes is listed on the membership section of the home page, and it changes only when we say so on this page, with 30 days' notice to existing members.

  • Seeker (free) — the daily reflection, the practices, the community, the Forty Days, the public archive.
  • Supporter (£5/month) — the reading room, the live gatherings, the library.
  • Circle (£9.99/month) — everything above, the Scriptorium, quarterly one-to-one guidance, and the right to steward a circle once you reach that rank.

What membership is not

It is not a qualification, a ministry, a licence to teach, or a claim of spiritual authority. Nobody here is your guru, and paying more does not make you further along.

Circles

A circle is a licensed local gathering. A steward keeps 75% of the service fees the circle charges for its meetings and remits 25% to The Lotus Cross for the licence, materials and support. A circle may not collect donations in our name, may not describe itself as a charity, and may not promise spiritual or material outcomes for money. Stewards are independent and are not our employees.

Practices & ranks

Practices are things you do, not things you buy. A rank is a record of how much practice you have done here — nothing more. It is not ordination, not a title to use outside this site, and it confers no authority over another member.

Some practices ask for a photograph as evidence. A person reviews it, and it is used only to confirm the practice happened. Images are deleted after 180 days. You can ask for one to be deleted sooner, at any time.

Conduct

Three things are not tolerated, and they are the only three that lead to immediate removal:

  • Using this place to sell. No pitches, no funnels, no “DM me for my course”, no recruiting into another organisation.
  • Claiming authority over another person. No spiritual direction by force, no “I have been sent to guide you”, no pressure to give money, no secrecy.
  • Cruelty. No shaming, no conversion contests between traditions, no targeting anyone for their background, body, faith or lack of it.

Beyond that, argue freely. This community was built on traditions that disagree with each other; disagreement is the point, contempt is not.

Moderation

A human reads reports. We may remove a message, mute an account, or end a membership. If we end a paid membership for conduct, we refund the unused period.

Care & safeguarding

This is not therapy, and not a crisis service. Nothing here diagnoses, treats or replaces medical or psychological care. If you are in danger or in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line in your country — in the UK, Samaritans on 116 123, any hour.

We keep anything shared in confidence unless someone is at risk. If a member discloses abuse or a risk to a child or vulnerable adult, we act on it, including contacting the authorities where the law requires.

Members under 18 are not admitted. If we learn an account belongs to a minor, it is closed and any payment refunded.

Live gatherings are not recorded unless every person present is told and agrees before recording starts.

Your content

What you write here stays yours. You give us permission to display it to the community and to store it so the site works — nothing more. We do not sell it, train models on it, or publish it outside the community without asking you.

The public archive links to texts in the public domain. Translations carry their own rights, which is why we link to the editions rather than copying them.

Complaints

Write to hello@thelotuscross.org with “complaint” in the subject. A human answers within five working days, and we tell you what we are going to do even when the answer is no.

If we cannot resolve it, you may use the EU/UK online dispute resolution platform, or complain to your local trading standards service. We would rather hear it first.

Accessibility

The site is built to work with a keyboard, a screen reader and a phone with a small screen. Text is resizable to 200% without breaking the layout, and motion stops if your device asks it to. If something is unusable for you, tell us and we will fix that specific thing — accessibility is not a project we finished, it is a queue.

Who we are

The Lotus Cross is run by one person in the United Kingdom, with help from a small number of members. Contact, including for data protection requests: hello@thelotuscross.org.
