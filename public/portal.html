Path of Ascension

The Five Ranks

Your rank reflects your commitment, not your worth. Advance by completing daily tasks and embodying the teachings.

How to Participate

The Member's Guide

🌱 Getting Started (Seeker)

  1. Complete your daily tasks. Log in daily and complete the 5 sacred tasks. Each earns XP and advances your rank.
  2. Maintain your streak. Consecutive days build a streak. Breaks reset it but not your progress.
  3. Study the teachings. Read the Three Paths on the main site. Reflect on how they connect.
  4. Live the values. Practice love, awareness, and righteous action in everything you do.

🌿 Advancing to Initiate (Rank 1)

  • Complete 7 daily tasks
  • Take the Vow of Harmony
  • Begin mentoring newer Seekers

🔥 Becoming a Disciple (Rank 2)

  • Complete 30+ daily tasks
  • Lead a study circle
  • Organise a community service event
  • Begin teaching the basics

⭐ Rising to Elder (Rank 3)

  • Complete 90+ daily tasks
  • Serve as a community guide
  • Represent us in interfaith dialogue
  • Be a living example

✦ Reaching Guide (Rank 4)

  • The highest rank, earned through years of devoted service
  • Guides shape the community's direction
  • Selected by consensus of Guides and Elders
Daily Practice

What the Tasks Mean

The Five Daily Tasks

  • Meditation (10 minutes) — The Way of Awareness. Sit still. Breathe. Observe your mind.
  • Act of Kindness — The Way of Love. One deliberate act of kindness for another.
  • Scripture Reading — Read a passage from any sacred text. Reflect on its meaning.
  • Mindful Action — The Way of Order. Perform one task with full mindful awareness.
  • Self-Reflection — At day's end, reflect on what you did well and what karma you created.

Community Values

How We Treat Each Other

  • Respect all paths. No tradition is superior.
  • No proselytising. Share experience; do not impose beliefs.
  • Confidentiality. What is shared stays in the community.
  • Kindness first. Disagree without disrespect.
  • Service over status. Ranks are for organisation, not hierarchy.
  • Voluntary participation. You may leave at any time.
