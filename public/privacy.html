Privacy Notice

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Version 1.0

The Lotus Cross is a small, independent spiritual community. This notice explains, in plain language, what we hold about you and why. It is written to meet the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who is responsible. The Lotus Cross (“we”, “us”) is the data controller. Contact: privacy@thelotuscross.org. Postal address: available on request by email.

Sensitive information

Your religious or philosophical beliefs are special category data under Article 9 UK GDPR. We only hold this because you chose to tell us the tradition you feel closest to and because you joined a spiritual community. We rely on your explicit consent for this. You can ask us to delete it at any time, and you can still be a member without it.

2. Our lawful bases

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a)) — for joining, for the tradition you share, for photographs, and for the daily reflection email. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to give you the account and the community you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — to keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix faults. We have weighed this against your rights and keep these records minimal.

4. Who else sees it

We do not sell your data, and we do not advertise. We use a small number of service providers who process data on our instructions:

  • Render (hosting) — serves the website
  • Supabase (database) — stores accounts, practices and photographs
  • Resend (email) — delivers account emails and the daily reflection
  • Cloudflare (DNS and network) — routes traffic to the site

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where that happens, transfers rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or an adequacy decision. Write to us if you want the specific safeguards.

Other members can see your first name, your country, your rank and your practice count on the leaderboard, and your messages in the channels where you post them. They cannot see your email address unless you send it to them.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • be told what we hold, and to receive a copy (access)
  • have errors corrected (rectification)
  • have your data deleted (erasure) — “the right to be forgotten”
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was lawful before
  • complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113)

To exercise any of these, email privacy@thelotuscross.org. We answer within 30 days. We will never charge you for a request.

6. Photographs — please read

Practices that require evidence ask for a photograph of the act or the place, not of people's faces. When you upload a picture, it is compressed in your browser and stored without location data. Do not upload images of other people without their permission. If you do, you are responsible for having that permission, and we will remove the image on request.

7. Children

The community is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly hold data about children. If you believe a minor has enrolled, write to us and we will remove the account.

8. Security

Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Connections are encrypted. Access to the admin panel requires a password and, where enabled, a second factor. Evidence photographs are visible only to you, to Guides and Stewards, and to administrators.

9. Changes

If this notice changes in a way that affects you, we will email members before the change takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.

Plain summary: we hold what is needed to run a small community — your name, email, practice history and any photographs you choose to send. No advertising, no selling, photographs deleted after six months, and you can ask for everything to be erased at any time.
