What we do not sell. No tea, no herbs, no supplements, no incense — anything you eat, drink or burn indoors carries food-safety and labelling duties that a small shop should not be learning on your money. And no object here is sold as lucky. A mark is not a charm: it promises nothing, changes nothing, protects you from nothing. It is there so that you remember something you already decided. A shop that tells you otherwise is selling you fear.
