2. What this is — and what it is not

The Lotus Cross is a community for practice, not a medical, psychological or financial service. Nothing here is therapy, diagnosis, legal advice or a promise of any outcome. If you are in crisis, contact your GP or a crisis line — in the UK, Samaritans on 116 123, any time.

We do not promise spiritual results, healing, prosperity or personal transformation. We offer practices and company. What you make of them is yours.

3. How to behave

  • Speak to others as you would want to be spoken to. Disagreement is welcome; contempt is not.
  • No proselytising against another member's tradition, and no using this community to recruit for another group, business or cause.
  • No harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual content, or content that glorifies self-harm.
  • Do not give medical, legal or financial advice to other members as if you were qualified to give it.
  • Do not share another member's words, photographs or story outside the community without their permission.

We may remove a message, suspend an account, or close it, if these rules are broken. Where possible you will be told why and given a chance to respond.

4. Evidence photographs

  • Photograph the act or the place, not other people's faces.
  • Do not upload anything you do not have the right to share.
  • Photographs are reviewed by a Guide or Steward, kept for 180 days, and then deleted.
  • Faking evidence (stock photos, someone else's picture, a photograph of an act you did not do) is a breach of these terms. It may end your membership.

5. Ranks, circles and money

  • A Steward keeps 75% of what their circle receives and remits 25% to The Lotus Cross as a licence fee for the name and materials. Declarations are made honestly, monthly, in the portal.
  • The Steward is responsible for their own tax and for complying with local law where the circle meets.
  • We do not operate a franchise, an investment, or a scheme in which you earn from recruiting other people.
